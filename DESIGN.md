# Design sheet
**Loop:** lobby (pick character, ready) > boss 1..4 (Jogo, Hanami, Mahito, Sukuna) > perk draft between bosses (each player picks 1 of 3; team perks affect everyone) > win or wipe > lobby.
**Resources:** CE builds from dealing (+3) and taking damage (+0.6/hp), +2/s passive, +25 on Black Flash. Ultimates cost 100.
**Look:** paper-white panel, halftone vignette, hatched floor bands, thick panel border. Colour only from cursed energy (crimson Itadori, jade Fushiguro, lime Ieiri, electric blue Gojo, neon purple curses, crimson Sukuna). Heavy hits spawn kanji/English SFX; impact frames invert the canvas with speed lines. Domains crack the page open with a jagged black edge and flood it with a full-colour world.
**Domains:** one at a time. Casting into an active boss domain (or the boss casting into yours) starts a 4s Domain Clash mash; the winner's domain takes over, the loser takes damage/stun.
